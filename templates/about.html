Who We Are

Welcome to Surabhi Cow Care Sanctuary, a compassionate haven dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation, and lifelong care of stray, abandoned, and old cows. For us, cow care is not just a duty—it is a sacred responsibility.

We believe every living being deserves to live free from suffering. Our sanctuary provides a permanent, stress-free home where injured or neglected cattle receive medical attention, nutritious fodder, and endless affection.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To rescue abandoned and distressed cattle, provide them with immediate veterinary care, and offer a sustainable, safe environment where they can live with absolute dignity.

Our Vision

To create a world where cows are no longer abandoned or mistreated, and to promote organic, cow-centric agriculture that benefits both humanity and nature.

What We Do

Emergency Rescue

Operating dedicated ambulances to rescue injured or accident-prone cows from streets.

Nutritious Diet

Providing freshly sourced green fodder, grains, and clean drinking water daily.

Medical Ward

Equipped with resident veterinarians to handle routine health checks and surgeries.
